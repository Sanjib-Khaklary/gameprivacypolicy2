Privacy Policy

Fight Game (Android App)

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fight Game ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile game application (the "App") available on Google Play.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Fight Game is designed as a standalone action fighting game that respects user privacy. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal identifying information (such as your name, physical address, or phone number), and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Ad Services
Fight Game uses Google AdMob to display in-game banner advertisements and rewarded video advertisements (which allow players to earn in-game coins, revives, or bonuses). To serve and optimize advertisements, Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect certain device and usage data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID / AD_ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language settings)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address at the country/city level; we do not access precise GPS location)
  • Ad interaction metrics (ad impressions, clicks, load performance)

B. Local Game Progress (On-Device Storage Only)
All gameplay progress, high scores, coin balances, unlocked characters, and settings are stored locally on your device using Android local storage (Room Database and Preferences). This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any private external game servers.

2. How We Use the Information

The information collected through third-party services is used solely for:

  • Displaying banner and rewarded video ads that support the ongoing maintenance and free access to the App.
  • Preventing fraudulent ad traffic and ensuring ad service security.
  • Analyzing aggregate gameplay metrics to optimize game balance, combat performance, and fix crashes.
  • Saving and restoring your fighting stats, character selections, and match records locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Our App integrates third-party services provided by Google LLC. These services operate under their own independent privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads SDK: Used to display in-game advertising.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Ad Technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Services: Core platform services provided by the Android OS.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Device Permissions

Fight Game requests only standard network permissions necessary for basic app features and ad delivery:

  • INTERNET: Allows the App to connect to the network to request and render banner and rewarded video ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the App to verify whether an active internet connection is available before attempting to load an advertisement.

Fight Game does not request or access camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, SMS, or external storage files.

5. Your Choices & Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  • Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

6. Children's Privacy

Fight Game is an arcade fighting action game intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Third-party ad networks used in the App are configured in compliance with applicable children's privacy laws, including COPPA and Google Play Families Policies. If you believe that personal information has inadvertently been collected from a child, please contact us at the email below so we can take immediate corrective measures.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because gameplay data (high scores, unlocked fighters, coins) is stored strictly on your local device, you have complete control over it. You can delete all local data at any time by clearing the App's storage via Settings > Apps > Fight Game > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the App.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
